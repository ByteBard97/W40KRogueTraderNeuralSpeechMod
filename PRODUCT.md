# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One person: the mod's owner. They sit at a desk in the evening, in headphones, in a dim room, looping a few seconds of a generated voice and turning controls until it sounds right. They cannot use light mode at all. They know audio vocabulary (phaser, ring mod, comb, formant) and want precise control, not hand-holding.

## Product Purpose

Voice Studio is the local workbench for the Rogue Trader neural speech mod. The FX Lab is the part where a character's post-processing (machine, vox-caster, ghost, daemon) is tuned by ear and saved as a preset that a batch script then applies to every rendered line of that character. A setting is only useful if the script can reproduce it exactly.

## Brand Personality

Calm, exact, instrument-like. A studio rack at night, not a dashboard.

## Anti-references

- Anything bright or white. Light mode is unusable for this user.
- A wall of every control at once. If 100 knobs are on screen, none of them is findable.
- Decoration that competes with the listening: glow, gradients, animated chrome.
- Generic SaaS card grids.

## Design Principles

1. Dark only, low glare, no pure white. Text and controls stay readable for hours.
2. Show what is in use. Effects that are off or untouched stay out of the way and one click away.
3. One thing in focus at a time: the clip, the effect being tuned, and the comparison against the real game voice.
4. Every control maps to a parameter the batch script can set. No UI-only state.
5. Hearing comes first: play, bypass, and A/B are always reachable without scrolling.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Dark theme only, with comfortable contrast (not maximum contrast on pure black). Controls usable by keyboard and mouse wheel. No motion that is not tied to the audio.
